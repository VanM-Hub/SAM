\# Purpose



This document defines the strategic direction of the SAM 2.x development phase.



SAM 1.x established the identity of the project.



SAM 2.x realizes that identity as an operational governance platform.



This document does not redefine the Foundation.



It defines how the Foundation shall be realized.



\---



\# Scope



This strategy governs every architectural and engineering activity performed during the SAM 2.x lifecycle.



It applies to:



\- Software Architecture

\- Engineering

\- Runtime realization

\- Repository evolution

\- Operational readiness

\- Release planning



It does not modify any constitutional document.



\---



\# Strategic Mission



SAM 2.x exists to transform the constitutional architecture of SAM into a fully operational governance platform.



The objective is not to invent new architecture.



The objective is to realize the existing architecture.



\---



\# North Star



SAM 2.x is not about building more Runtimes.



It is about making the existing architecture operational.



Every engineering decision should move the platform closer to production readiness.



\---



\# Development Philosophy



Foundation defines identity.



Development realizes identity.



Implementation serves architecture.



Architecture serves governance.



Governance serves the Mission.



This relationship shall remain unchanged throughout SAM 2.x.



\---



\# Strategic Objectives



SAM 2.x pursues five strategic objectives.



\## 1. Governance Operational



Every constitutional governance component shall become operational.



Including:



\- Mission

\- Workflow

\- Policy

\- Registry

\- Approval

\- Execution

\- Verification

\- Audit



Governance shall function as one complete operational chain.



\---



\## 2. Production Real Execution



Preview-only execution shall evolve into production-ready execution.



Execution shall remain:



\- governed

\- deterministic

\- auditable

\- approval-gated



Real Execution shall never bypass constitutional governance.



\---



\## 3. Evidence-Based Operational Learning



Operational experience shall become governed knowledge.



Learning shall be derived from:



\- execution evidence

\- audit history

\- operational metrics

\- simulation results



Learning shall never modify constitutional principles.



\---



\## 4. Single-Node Production



SAM shall become deployable as a complete governance platform on a single production node.



Installation,



configuration,



provider integration,



mission execution,



audit,



and monitoring



shall operate without modifying the Foundation.



\---



\## 5. Early Adopter Experience



SAM shall become usable by early adopters.



Priority users include:



\- Automation Engineers

\- AI Engineers

\- Workflow Engineers

\- Enterprise Architects

\- Research Organizations



Developer experience shall become part of the platform.



\---



\# Engineering Principles



Engineering during SAM 2.x follows these principles.



\## Realization Before Expansion



Existing architecture shall be completed before new architectural concepts are introduced.



\---



\## Operational Before Additional



Existing capabilities shall become operational before new capabilities are added.



\---



\## Integration Before Creation



Whenever possible,



existing Runtime,



Capability,



or Service



shall be extended rather than creating new architectural components.



\---



\## Production Before Optimization



Correctness,



trust,



and governance



have priority over performance optimization.



\---



\## Evidence Before Assumption



Engineering decisions shall be supported by measurable evidence whenever practical.



\---



\# Runtime Evolution Policy



During SAM 2.x,



existing Runtime implementations may evolve freely.



Including:



\- internal APIs

\- package structure

\- repository layout

\- deployment model

\- implementation strategy



The following shall remain unchanged:



\- Mission

\- Constitution

\- Philosophy

\- Governance

\- Canonical Architecture

\- Fundamental ADR



\---



\# Architectural Rule



No new Runtime shall be introduced unless required by the Constitution.



New Runtime creation requires architectural justification demonstrating that:



\- an independent governance responsibility exists;



\- an existing Runtime cannot reasonably own that responsibility;



\- constitutional isolation provides measurable architectural benefit.



Otherwise,



the capability shall remain inside an existing Runtime.



\---



\# Success Measurement



Progress shall be measured through platform readiness.



Not by:



\- runtime count

\- source code size

\- sprint count

\- number of modules



Primary indicators are:



\- Governance Readiness

\- Operational Readiness

\- Production Readiness

\- Developer Readiness

\- Platform Readiness



\---



\# Definition of Done



SAM 2.0 is complete when an organization can install a single-node SAM instance,



connect real providers,



execute an end-to-end Mission through constitutional governance,



obtain complete audit evidence,



receive evidence-based operational recommendations,



and operate the system reliably,



without requiring any modification to the Foundation.



\---



\# Transition to SAM 3.0



SAM 2.x concludes when SAM operates as a production-ready governance platform.



SAM 3.x begins only after that objective has been achieved.



The focus of SAM 3.x shifts from platform realization to ecosystem expansion.



Future ecosystem capabilities may include:



\- SDK

\- Plugin ecosystem

\- Third-party Citizens

\- Provider ecosystem

\- Community extensions



These belong to SAM 3.x,



not SAM 2.x.



\---



\# Final Statement



SAM 1.x defined what SAM is.



SAM 2.x proves that SAM works.



Every engineering effort during this phase should reduce the distance between constitutional architecture and operational reality.



The Foundation remains unchanged.



The Platform becomes real.
