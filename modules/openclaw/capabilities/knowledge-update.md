# Purpose of Knowledge Update



Knowledge Update promotes validated operational experience into reusable organizational knowledge.



Promotion shall occur only after sufficient supporting evidence exists.



---



# Scope



Knowledge Update may evaluate:



\- operational patterns

\- verified recommendations

\- repeated diagnostic findings

\- recurring provider behavior

\- recurring model behavior

\- recurring configuration outcomes

\- execution trends



Knowledge Update shall not modify runtime behavior.



---

Each stage shall preserve references to previous stages.



---



# Knowledge Candidate



A Knowledge Candidate represents a proposed knowledge item awaiting validation.



Each candidate should include:



\- candidate identifier

\- supporting evidence

\- related execution history

\- related operational patterns

\- confidence

\- review status



Knowledge Candidates are not yet trusted knowledge.



---



# Promotion Requirements



Knowledge shall only be promoted when:



\- evidence is sufficient

\- observations are repeatable

\- patterns are stable

\- contradictory evidence is evaluated

\- confidence satisfies TRUST\_MODEL requirements



Failure to satisfy these requirements shall retain the item as a Knowledge Candidate.



---



# Validation Criteria



Validation should evaluate:



\- evidence quality

\- evidence quantity

\- reproducibility

\- operational relevance

\- confidence level



Validation shall be documented.



---



# Confidence Evolution



Confidence may increase through:



\- repeated confirmation

\- independent observations

\- successful verification



Confidence may decrease through:



\- contradictory evidence

\- failed verification

\- operational changes



Knowledge confidence is dynamic.



---



# Relationship to Memory



Memory preserves operational experience.



Knowledge Update evaluates operational experience.



Knowledge preserves validated understanding.



Memory and Knowledge shall remain distinct.



---



# Relationship to Recommendations



Recommendations remain advisory.



Knowledge may later incorporate validated recommendations if sufficient evidence accumulates.



Recommendation alone does not establish knowledge.



---



# Knowledge Lifecycle



Knowledge progresses through:



Candidate



↓



Validated



↓



Active



↓



Reviewed



↓



Deprecated



↓



Archived



Historical knowledge shall remain traceable.



---



# Operational Boundaries



Knowledge Update shall not:



\- delete historical evidence

\- rewrite execution history

\- discard contradictory observations

\- automatically modify operational procedures



Knowledge promotion shall remain evidence-driven.
