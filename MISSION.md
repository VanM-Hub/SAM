# SAM Mission

Version: 2.0.0

Status: Accepted

Owner: Project SAM

---

# Mission Statement

The mission of SAM is to provide a trustworthy governance platform for intelligent systems.

Rather than creating intelligence, SAM governs how intelligence is discovered, selected, coordinated, approved, executed, audited, and evolved.

SAM exists to ensure that intelligence remains accountable regardless of how technology changes.

---

# Purpose

SAM exists to solve one fundamental problem:

Modern intelligent systems evolve faster than their governance.

Models improve.

Agents become more autonomous.

Providers multiply.

Capabilities expand.

Without governance, intelligent systems become increasingly difficult to understand, trust, and control.

SAM exists to restore that balance.

---

# Core Responsibilities

SAM is responsible for governing the complete lifecycle of intelligence.

This includes:

- Mission definition
- Workflow governance
- Capability discovery
- Runtime coordination
- Agent orchestration
- Policy enforcement
- Approval management
- Execution governance
- Audit generation
- Artifact governance
- Trust evaluation
- Long-term evolution

SAM does not own intelligence.

SAM governs intelligence.

---

# What SAM Governs

SAM governs interactions among constitutional Citizens.

Including:

- Runtimes
- Agents
- Models
- Providers
- Connectors
- Skills
- Tools
- Workflows
- Missions
- Policies
- Artifacts

Every governed interaction should be:

- deterministic where governance is concerned
- explainable
- auditable
- certifiable
- replaceable
- evolvable

---

# What SAM Does Not Do

SAM is not:

- an AI model
- a chatbot
- an LLM
- a provider
- a workflow engine
- a scheduler
- a database
- an orchestration library

Those may exist inside the ecosystem.

SAM governs how they cooperate.

---

# Human-Centered Governance

Human authority remains fundamental.

No autonomous execution should bypass constitutional approval.

Automation exists to reduce effort.

It never removes accountability.

Every significant action should remain attributable.

---

# Provider Independence

SAM should never depend on a single vendor.

Providers are replaceable.

Models are replaceable.

Execution engines are replaceable.

The governance model is not.

---

# Runtime Responsibility

Each Runtime owns one bounded governance responsibility.

Examples include:

- Memory Runtime
- Knowledge Runtime
- Workflow Runtime
- Policy Runtime
- Audit Runtime
- Execution Runtime

A Runtime owns governance.

It does not own the entire platform.

---

# Capability-Oriented Collaboration

Every Citizen collaborates through published Capabilities.

Citizens should never rely on implementation knowledge.

Capabilities become the universal language of cooperation.

---

# Constitutional Evolution

SAM should continuously evolve.

New Runtime types may appear.

New Citizens may appear.

New Providers may appear.

New deployment topologies may appear.

The Constitution should remain stable.

Identity should outlive implementation.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective of SAM is to become the constitutional operating layer for heterogeneous intelligent ecosystems.

An ecosystem where:

multiple models,

multiple agents,

multiple providers,

multiple runtimes,

multiple organizations,

and future forms of intelligence

can cooperate safely through shared governance.

---

# Success Criteria

SAM succeeds when:

Intelligence becomes replaceable without losing governance.

Execution becomes safer through approval.

Every important decision becomes explainable.

Every execution becomes auditable.

Every capability becomes discoverable.

Every Citizen becomes certifiable.

Every architectural evolution preserves constitutional identity.

---

# Mission Summary

Project SAM exists to establish a universal governance model that enables trustworthy cooperation among intelligent systems through constitutional principles, deterministic coordination, immutable contracts, and long-term architectural stability.

---

# Final Statement

Technology changes.

Governance endures.

SAM is committed to ensuring that intelligent systems remain trustworthy regardless of how rapidly intelligence evolves.
